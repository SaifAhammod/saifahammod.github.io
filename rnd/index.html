Who is Saif?

Saif is a Engineer, Roboticist, Businessman, and A Researcher from Rajshahi, Bangladesh, where he was also born and raised.
Saif holds a BSc in Computer Science and Engineering, which he completed with Magna Cum Laude honors. He is currently pursuing an MSc in Computer Science and Engineering at North South University, where his thesis research is ongoing.
Saif's early career reflects a strong foundation in both entrepreneurship and academia. He served as CTO and Co-Founder of HopIn, an Android-based ride-sharing platform designed for students. Alongside this venture, he co-founded NSU Ignite, a robotics team through which he represented his university in numerous robotics competitions across Bangladesh.
In parallel, Saif contributed to academia as a Graduate Teaching Assistant in the Department of ECE at North South University and as a Research Assistant at the NSU Intelligent Robotics Lab (NIRO Lab), where his work earned multiple awards.
Saif currently oversees his family enterprise, Green Hardware & Machineries, under which he established a new branch, Green Enterprise, expanding the business's reach and operations.
Beyond his business responsibilities, Saif remains deeply engaged in advancing Bangladesh's AI and robotics ecosystem through three interconnected initiatives: Codatron, ARC-BD, and Creator Lab.
Codatron is a platform envisioned to solve societal problems in Bangladesh through AI and robotics, foster local research and development, generate technology-driven employment, and train the next generation of engineers. ARC-BD, recognized as Bangladesh's first AI and Robotics community, works to build a collaborative, youth-driven innovation ecosystem in the country. Supporting both efforts is Creator Lab, a dedicated facility that provides infrastructure and resources for Codatron's mission.
Outside his professional pursuits, Saif is an avid photographer, reader, and graphic design enthusiast. He enjoys video games, movies, and series, and maintains a strong passion for football, both as an active player (amateur) and a devoted fan.
